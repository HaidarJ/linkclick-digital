## What does a paid social agency do?

A paid social agency plans, builds and runs paid advertising on social platforms such as Facebook, Instagram and TikTok, then improves it from the results. Linkclick Digital runs Meta and TikTok ads for founder-led businesses in many industries, supplying the creative as well as the media buying, and judges every campaign on booked calls and qualified enquiries.

The work covers strategy (the offer, the audience and what a good month looks like), creative, media buying with conversion tracking, and reporting that shows what your spend bought.

Paid social is one of four channels in Linkclick Digital's [digital marketing service](/services/digital-marketing/). UK social media adspend rose 21% to £11.5bn in 2025, and video made up 59% of it ([IAB UK](https://www.iabuk.com/news-article/digital-adspend-2025-uks-digital-ad-market-reaches-ps405bn)), so every account we run comes with a steady supply of short-form video.

## Why creative now drives results on Meta

Creative drives results on Meta because Meta's AI chooses which ads each person sees, and more varied creative gives it more options to work with. Meta says advertisers who supply genuinely different creative can get more reach, personalisation and performance. Its retrieval system, Andromeda, scans tens of millions of ads to pick a few thousand strong candidates.

Those points come from Meta's [December 2025 guide to creative diversification](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/demystifying-creative-diversification). It treats ads that look or feel alike as variations of the same creative, so a new headline or thumbnail adds little, and recommends ads that differ in look, story and message. Relevance also counts in the auction: [a more relevant ad can beat ads with higher bids](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/430291176997542).

So the brief now matters more than the bid. A founder explaining the service, a customer describing their experience and a team member answering a common question are three different ads, not three versions of one. That is what our human-led [UGC ad creative](/services/ugc-content/) is for: real people on camera, with AI helping us edit, caption and cut versions faster.

## Is TikTok ads or Meta ads better?

Neither platform is better for every business. Linkclick Digital usually starts founder-led service businesses on Meta (Facebook and Instagram), then adds TikTok when you can supply native, quick-cut video and your buyers already use the app. If your industry has extra ad rules, each platform's policy is checked first. The deciding test is cost per qualified enquiry.

| Your situation | Where Linkclick Digital usually starts |
|---|---|
| A founder-led service business | Meta (Facebook and Instagram) |
| You can supply native, quick-cut video and your buyers already use TikTok | Both, tested side by side with the same concepts |
| Your industry has extra ad rules on one platform | The platform whose policy fits your ads, confirmed before launch |

## How we test creative

Linkclick Digital tests paid social creative in monthly rounds. A few genuinely different concepts go live together, each with its own opening hook, and the results decide whether each one gets more budget, gets new versions or is switched off. The aim is a steady flow of new ideas, not endless tweaks to one ad.

1. **Pick the angles.** From your customers' real questions we choose distinct concepts: the founder on the service, a customer story, an objection answered.
2. **Script and check.** Scripts are checked against the platform's ad policies, the advertising codes where your ads will run (in the UK, the ASA and CAP rules) and any rules your industry has, before filming.
3. **Film with real people.** Customers, vetted creators or your team appear on camera. AI helps with editing, captions and cut-downs.
4. **Launch with clean tracking.** Each concept gets a fair share of spend, with conversions tracked to the enquiry or booking.
5. **Read the results.** Cost per qualified enquiry comes first. Hook rate (how many stop scrolling) and hold rate (how many keep watching) explain why.
6. **Scale, rework or cut.** Winners get more budget and new versions. Weak concepts are replaced next round.

## What paid social costs

Paid social costs two separate things: the ad spend you pay Meta or TikTok, and the management fee you pay an agency for strategy, creative and running the account. You control the ad spend through the budget you set. Because relevance is part of the auction, stronger creative can make that budget go further. Linkclick Digital doesn't publish fixed fees.

The management fee depends on:

- how many platforms you run
- how many new creative concepts you need each month, and who films them
- tracking and CRM set-up
- checks against your industry's advertising rules, if it has them
- how detailed the reporting needs to be

Share a budget range when you [book a strategy call](/contact/) and we will tell you honestly what it can achieve.

## Ads in industries with extra rules

Ads in industries with extra rules face two layers of checks on Meta and TikTok: each platform's own ad policies, and the laws and advertising codes where the ads run. Both platforms restrict some categories of ads, and in some markets certain industries need approval before their ads can run. Linkclick Digital checks both layers before launch.

- **Meta:** its [Advertising Standards](https://transparency.meta.com/en-gb/policies/ad-standards/) say each ad is reviewed against Meta's policies, and that advertisers are responsible for following those policies as well as local laws, regulations and, where they apply, self-regulatory advertising codes.
- **TikTok:** its [advertising policies](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-advertising-policies) list the restricted categories and link to market-specific requirements for ad approval.

Linkclick Digital is not a law firm: we build these checks into planning, scripts and targeting, and you keep final sign-off.
