## What does a social media management agency do?

A social media management agency runs a business's organic social channels: it plans what to post, makes or sources the content, publishes it, answers comments and messages, and reports on what works. Linkclick Digital does this for founder-led businesses in many industries, with the content built from human-led UGC and judged on enquiries, not likes.

Linkclick Digital treats your profile as one step on the path from first view to booked call. Someone who sees your ad or finds you in search, then checks your Instagram, should find recent, real proof and an obvious way to get in touch.

If your industry has advertising rules, every caption, script and hashtag is checked against them before it goes live, and you keep final sign-off.

## What's included

Linkclick Digital's social media management covers five parts: a strategy and monthly content calendar, content production from human-led UGC, scheduling and posting, community management, and monthly reporting. Each part is scoped to the channels you use and how much content you need, and it all runs from the same plan as your paid and search work.

### Strategy and content calendar

We agree what each channel is for, who it speaks to and which questions it should answer, then plan a month of posts ahead so you can review them before anything is published.

### Content production

Short-form video and images come from our [UGC and creator content service](/services/ugc-content/): real customers, vetted creators and your founders on camera. AI helps us edit, caption and resize faster.

### Scheduling and posting

Posts go out on a set schedule, formatted for each platform, with captions, hashtags and links checked against any advertising rules your industry has.

### Community management

We reply to comments and messages in your voice during agreed hours, and pass anything sensitive to your team. For a faster first reply, we can connect an AI assistant that will [auto-reply to DMs](/services/ai-automation/) with answers you approve, with a person in the loop.

### Reporting

One monthly report covers reach, but leads with what matters more: profile visits, link clicks, messages and the enquiries they became.

## How much does a social media manager cost?

In the UK, an in-house social media manager earns an average of £25,000 a year at starter level and £50,000 when experienced, according to the [National Careers Service](https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/job-profiles/social-media-manager). The full cost of a hire is higher once you add employer costs, software and the filming and editing one person rarely has time to do alone.

So compare the whole cost, not the salary, with any agency quote. An agency fee depends on different things:

- **Channels:** how many platforms you need managed.
- **Content volume:** how many posts and videos each month, and who films them.
- **Community management:** how many hours of replies, and how fast they need to be.
- **Compliance:** review time, if your industry has advertising rules.
- **Reporting:** how much detail you need, and whether it joins up with your ads and website data.

Paid boosting of posts is ad spend, which sits on top and goes to the platform. Linkclick Digital doesn't publish package prices, because these factors vary so much. Share a budget range when you [book a strategy call](/contact/) and we will tell you honestly what it can achieve.

## Organic, paid and UGC: which page does what

Organic social, paid social and UGC do three different jobs. UGC is the content itself, made with real people. Social media management publishes it to your own followers and handles the conversations. Paid social puts the best of it in front of new people and measures what each enquiry costs. Linkclick Digital runs all three from one plan.

| Service | Its job | What it is judged on |
|---|---|---|
| [UGC and creator content](/services/ugc-content/) | Makes the videos, with real people on camera | Whether people stop, watch and act |
| Social media management (this page) | Publishes to your followers and answers comments and messages | Profile visits, messages and enquiries |
| [Paid social on Meta and TikTok](/services/digital-marketing/paid-social/) | Reaches new people with the strongest content | Cost per qualified enquiry |

The three feed each other. A post that does well with your followers becomes a candidate for a paid test, and an ad concept that wins becomes a post.
