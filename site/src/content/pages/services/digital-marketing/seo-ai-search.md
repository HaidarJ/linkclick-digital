## What is the difference between SEO and GEO?

SEO (search engine optimisation) helps your pages rank in search results. GEO (generative engine optimisation) helps AI assistants find, trust and cite them in their answers. Google treats them as one job: its May 2026 guide says optimising for its generative AI features is still SEO, and a page must be indexed and eligible to show in Google Search to appear in them.

That guidance is [Google's guide to optimising for generative AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide), announced on [Google's Search Central blog on 15 May 2026](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/05/a-new-resource-for-optimizing). It also says you don't need special AI files, markup or Markdown copies, that llms.txt won't help Search visibility, and that content needn't be split into small chunks.

Google's guide covers Google only, so Linkclick Digital also checks ChatGPT, Perplexity and Copilot.

Both halves matter. When an AI Overview appears, the top-ranking page's click-through rate is 58% lower on average ([Ahrefs, December 2025 against December 2023](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks-update)). Yet across 101,574 websites, organic search sent 42.75% of all traffic in 2026 and AI platforms sent 0.32% ([SE Ranking](https://seranking.com/blog/ai-traffic-research-study/)). So we sell SEO and GEO as one service: earn the ranking, then be the page AI answers quote.

## How AI assistants choose which businesses to name

There is no published formula for which businesses AI assistants name, so Linkclick Digital works on three things that make a business easier to recommend: being easy to identify, being described the same way by other sources, and having pages with passages worth quoting.

### One clear entity

Use the same business name, description, services and location on your website, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, directories and social bios. Conflicting facts can give an assistant a reason to hedge or leave you out. Google's guide says Google Business Profile can help your services show in AI responses as well as ordinary results.

### Corroboration from other sites

Reviews, directory listings, press coverage and podcast appearances tell assistants what others say about you. They have to be genuine. Google's guide says chasing inauthentic mentions does little good, and in the UK [fake reviews are a banned practice](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/online-reviews-and-endorsements-information-for-businesses) under consumer law that came into force in April 2025.

### Pages AI can quote

Each page should answer one question near the top, in a passage that still makes sense when quoted alone, with every number linked to its source. Google's guide favours first-hand experience over summaries of what is already published. Our guide on [how to get your business recommended by ChatGPT](/insights/get-recommended-by-chatgpt/) covers each step in more detail.

## How we measure AI visibility

Linkclick Digital measures AI visibility every month with a fixed set of prompts run in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Copilot, logging whether your business is named or cited and which page is linked. We pair that with Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools data, and with enquiries traced back to the page where they started.

1. **Agree the prompt set.** We write the questions your buyers actually ask, and keep them fixed so months can be compared.
2. **Run and log the prompts.** Each month we record, per assistant, whether you are named, whether you are cited and which URL is linked.
3. **Check Google's data.** Search Console's [generative AI performance reports](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports) show impressions in AI Overviews and AI Mode by page and country. Google says they reached all websites on 31 August 2026.
4. **Check Bing's data.** The [AI Performance report in Bing Webmaster Tools](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/February-2026/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview), in public preview since February 2026, counts citations in Microsoft Copilot and Bing's AI summaries, by page.
5. **Follow the enquiries.** AI referrals are tracked as their own channel in analytics, and every enquiry is attributed to its landing page.
6. **Report and adjust.** One monthly report shows rankings, AI mentions and enquiries together, and sets the next month's priorities.
