## What does a paid search (Google Ads) agency do?

A paid search agency sets up and runs ads that appear when people search for what you sell, mainly on Google Ads, and it is only worth paying for when those clicks become customers. Linkclick Digital handles keyword research, campaign builds, ad copy, landing pages and conversion tracking for founder-led businesses in many industries, then improves them every month.

Paid search catches people who are already looking. That makes it the natural partner to [paid social on Meta and TikTok](/services/digital-marketing/paid-social/), which reaches people who weren't searching yet. Both sit inside Linkclick Digital's [digital marketing service](/services/digital-marketing/), with one plan and one report.

Search remains one of the biggest parts of UK digital advertising. Search adspend grew 6% to £17.9bn in 2025, 44% of all UK digital adspend, according to [IAB UK's Digital Adspend 2025 figures](https://www.iabuk.com/news-article/digital-adspend-2025-uks-digital-ad-market-reaches-ps405bn).

Month to month, the work covers:

- **Keyword research** with Google's [Keyword Planner](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7337243?hl=en-GB), which estimates monthly searches and the average cost for an ad to show on each keyword.
- **Campaign structure** grouped by service and location, with negative keywords to keep out searches that won't convert.
- **Ads and assets** written to match the search and any advertising rules your industry has.
- **Conversion tracking** for calls, forms and bookings, so bids follow enquiries rather than clicks.
- **Search term reviews** to cut wasted spend and find new keywords.

## How much does Google Ads cost?

Google Ads costs whatever budget you set, because search ads are usually paid per click and Google caps spend against your average daily budget. On a single day Google can spend up to twice that daily figure, but across a month it won't charge more than 30.4 times it. Google's own example is a £10 daily budget with a £304 monthly maximum ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2375454?hl=en-GB)).

What each click costs is set by auction. Google's [Ad Rank](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722122?hl=en-GB) weighs your bid alongside the quality of your ad and landing page, the competition, the searcher's context and the expected impact of your ad assets. Google says higher-quality ads can often lead to lower costs per click. In practice, four things move your cost:

- **Competition:** the more advertisers bidding on a keyword, the higher the price.
- **Targeting:** where and when your ads show, and how tightly keywords match the search.
- **Quality:** how relevant your ad and landing page are to the search.
- **Conversion rate:** a page that turns more clicks into enquiries lowers your cost per enquiry, even at the same cost per click.

We don't quote typical cost-per-click ranges here, because they move with industry, location and season. Before launch, Linkclick Digital pulls estimates for your actual keywords from Keyword Planner and works back from the number of enquiries you need.

## How much should I pay someone to manage my Google Ads?

What you pay for Google Ads management should reflect the work involved, not just the size of your ad spend. The main drivers are how many campaigns and locations you run, whether landing pages and tracking need building, how often the account is reviewed and whether your industry's advertising rules need checking. Linkclick Digital scopes this on a strategy call.

Agencies tend to charge a percentage of ad spend, a fixed monthly fee or a mix of the two. A percentage rises with your budget even when the work doesn't. A fixed fee ties the cost to the scope.

Whoever manages your account, check three things before you sign:

- you own the Google Ads account and its data
- conversions are tracked to real enquiries, not page views
- you get a plain report every month that shows cost per enquiry

Linkclick Digital doesn't publish fixed prices. Share a budget range when you [book a strategy call](/contact/) and we will tell you honestly what it can achieve.

## Ads in industries with extra rules

Google Ads bans some kinds of advertising and restricts others, so industries with extra rules need a policy check before launch. Google's [advertising policies](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6008942?hl=en-GB) allow restricted content only on a limited basis: those ads may not show to every user in every location, and advertisers may need to meet additional requirements before their ads are eligible to run.

Google also requires all advertisers to follow local legal requirements when they use its products. So Linkclick Digital checks Google's policy for your category and your industry's advertising rules before launch, and builds keywords, ads and landing pages to fit them. Linkclick Digital is not a law firm, and you keep final sign-off on every ad and landing page.

## Landing pages that convert

A paid search landing page converts when it matches the search that brought the visitor, loads quickly on a phone and makes one next step obvious. Google also scores landing page quality as part of Ad Rank, so a better page can help lower your cost per click as well as raise the share of visitors who enquire.

What we build into each page:

- **One page per service or intent**, with a headline that echoes the search.
- **Proof near the top**, such as genuine reviews and the credentials of the person the visitor will meet.
- **One clear action**, a short form or a booking button, visible without scrolling.
- **A fast, mobile-first build**, so the page works on the phone in your customer's hand.
- **Tracking on every form, call and booking**, so Google Ads can bid on enquiries.

Linkclick Digital builds these pages through its [web design service](/services/web-design/). The same pages can also earn organic visits through [SEO and AI search](/services/digital-marketing/seo-ai-search/).
