## What does a digital marketing agency do?

A digital marketing agency plans, runs and measures the paid and organic channels that bring a business new customers: social ads, search ads, search engine optimisation (SEO) and social media. Linkclick Digital does this for founder-led businesses worldwide as one connected plan, judged on enquiries and booked calls rather than clicks or followers.

Agencies differ less in which channels they offer than in what they are accountable for: reach and clicks, or the enquiries and bookings those clicks turn into.

Clients are asking agencies for this mix. In [AgencyAnalytics' 2026 survey of 494 agency professionals](https://agencyanalytics.com/blog/agency-benchmarks-2026-ai-search), the services most often named for rising client demand were SEO for AI search (66%), performance-based paid ads (55%) and short-form video (51%).

Digital marketing is the middle of the Linkclick Digital system. [Human-led UGC and creator content](/services/ugc-content/) supplies the video the ads and posts run on, and [web design built for enquiries](/services/web-design/) gives the traffic somewhere to land. Planning all three together stops the most common leak: a good ad sending people to a slow page.

## The four channels we run

Linkclick Digital runs four digital marketing channels: paid social on Meta and TikTok, paid search (mainly Google Ads), SEO and AI search visibility, and social media management. Each has its own page with the detail; here is the job each one does in the system.

### Paid social on Meta and TikTok

Paid social puts your video in front of people who aren't searching yet but match your customer. We plan audiences, run Meta and TikTok campaigns and test new creative every month. UK social media ad spend grew 21% to £11.5bn in 2025, and video made up 59% of it ([IAB UK](https://www.iabuk.com/news-article/digital-adspend-2025-uks-digital-ad-market-reaches-ps405bn)). See [paid social advertising on Meta and TikTok](/services/digital-marketing/paid-social/).

### Paid search

Paid search reaches people at the moment they search for what you offer. We build the campaigns, keyword lists and conversion tracking, and send each click to a page built to turn it into an enquiry. Search is still the largest digital ad channel in the UK: spend grew 6% to £17.9bn in 2025, 44% of all digital ad spend ([IAB UK](https://www.iabuk.com/news-article/digital-adspend-2025-uks-digital-ad-market-reaches-ps405bn)). See [paid search and Google Ads management](/services/digital-marketing/paid-search/).

### SEO and AI search (GEO)

SEO and AI search visibility, also called GEO (generative engine optimisation), make your business easy for Google, Bing and AI assistants such as ChatGPT to find, understand and recommend. We sell them as one service, because AI assistants rely on search engines to find their sources.

Classic search still carries the traffic. Organic search sent 42.75% of website visits in 2026, against 0.32% from AI platforms, across 101,574 sites ([SE Ranking](https://seranking.com/blog/ai-traffic-research-study/)). But each click is harder to win: by December 2025, an AI Overview on the results page correlated with a 58% lower click-through rate for the top-ranking page ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks-update)). So your pages need to rank and to be the source an AI answer quotes. See [SEO and AI search visibility](/services/digital-marketing/seo-ai-search/), or read [how to get your business recommended by ChatGPT](/insights/get-recommended-by-chatgpt/).

### Social media management

Social media management keeps your own accounts active and answered: a content calendar, posting, replies to comments and messages, and a monthly report. It is built around human-led UGC, so your feed and your ads show the same real people. See [social media management for founder-led brands](/services/digital-marketing/social-media-management/).

## How much does a digital marketing agency cost?

The cost of a digital marketing agency depends on scope: how many channels it runs, how much creative it makes each month and the set-up work needed first. Ad spend, paid to Meta, TikTok or Google, sits on top of the management fee. Linkclick Digital scopes each plan after a strategy call instead of publishing fixed prices.

- **Channels.** Each channel adds planning, management and reporting time.
- **Creative volume.** Paid social needs a steady supply of new videos, hooks and ad versions. More new creative each month means more production.
- **Ad spend.** Your budget with the platforms is separate from the fee. Larger budgets usually mean more campaigns to manage and test.
- **Set-up.** Conversion tracking, landing pages, account clean-up or technical SEO fixes often come first.
- **Advertising rules.** If your industry has advertising rules, they add review time, because every ad and script is checked against them and goes through your sign-off before it goes live.

Choose a monthly budget band when you [book a strategy call](/contact/), and we'll tell you honestly what it can achieve.

## Are digital marketing agencies worth it?

A digital marketing agency is worth it when it is accountable for results you can count, such as enquiries and booked calls, and costs less than building the same skills in-house. It is rarely worth it for activity without a clear goal, or when nobody can show which channel produced which customer.

Doing it in-house means hiring or training for paid social, paid search, SEO, content and analytics, then keeping up as the platforms change. An agency already has those skills but knows your business less well at the start, so the first weeks should go on tracking and a plan, not on launching everything at once.

Before you hire any agency, ask:

- Who owns the ad accounts, the tracking data and the content?
- What does the monthly report measure, and how does it connect to bookings?
- Who will do the work day to day?
- How are ads and posts checked against any advertising rules in my industry?

## Meta ads vs Google Ads: which is better?

Neither Meta ads nor Google Ads is better in general, because they do different jobs. Google Ads reaches people who are already searching for what you sell, so it captures demand that exists. Meta ads, on Facebook and Instagram, reach people before they search, so they create demand, and they depend on strong video creative.

| Your situation | Start with | Why |
|---|---|---|
| People already search for what you sell | Google Ads | The ad and the landing page meet the search at the moment of need |
| Your service is new, or people don't know to search for it | Meta ads | Video can explain the problem to likely customers before they look for a solution |
| The sale depends on trust in a person, such as the founder | Meta ads, then Google Ads | Video of real people builds familiarity; search ads catch the people who later search for your name or service |
| You need enquiries from one local area | Google Ads | Search ads can be limited to the area you serve and show only when someone searches |
| You have a steady supply of good video or UGC | Meta ads | Meta campaigns need new creative to test, and you already have it |
| You have a small budget and no video yet | Google Ads, on a narrow set of searches | Text-based search ads can run while video is being made |

Often the answer is both, in sequence: Meta to build trust with video, Google to catch the searches that follow. Both platforms also have their own advertising policies, on top of any rules in your industry, and we plan every campaign around both: see [Meta and TikTok ads](/services/digital-marketing/paid-social/) and [Google Ads management](/services/digital-marketing/paid-search/).

## One plan, one report

Linkclick Digital runs every channel from one plan and reports on it in one monthly report, so you can see which channel produced which enquiry. The report leads with the numbers a founder cares about, enquiries, booked calls and cost per booked call, then shows the channel figures that explain them.

- **Outcomes:** enquiries, booked calls, cost per enquiry and cost per booked call, by channel and campaign.
- **Paid social:** which videos and hooks brought enquiries, the cost per result, and when an ad starts to tire.
- **Paid search:** the searches that brought enquiries, cost per enquiry and the wasted spend we cut.
- **SEO and AI search:** rankings and clicks, and whether AI assistants name your business in a fixed set of test prompts run each month.
- **Social media:** replies, profile visits and enquiries from messages. Reach is reported, but it isn't the target.

Each report changes the next month's plan: more budget for what brings bookings, new creative where ads tire, fixes where the website loses people. To trace every booking across the whole system, add [analytics and reporting](/add-ons/).
