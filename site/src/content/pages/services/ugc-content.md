## What does a UGC agency do?

A UGC agency plans, scripts, films and edits user-generated content for brands: short videos of real customers, creators or founders talking to camera like a personal recommendation. Linkclick Digital works for brands, not for creators looking for work. We find the right people, write the scripts and deliver edits ready for posts and ads.

Whatever you sell, the point of UGC is trust: a real person explaining, in plain words, what viewers want to know before they book a call.

A creator can film a good video alone. An agency adds everything around it: the brief, scripts checked against the advertising rules, usage rights agreed in writing, several edited versions, and a test that shows which one brings enquiries.

## What's included in UGC and creator content

Linkclick Digital's UGC service covers four kinds of work: customer and creator UGC, founder-led video, short-form editing and ad creative variations. They are planned together, so one round of filming feeds both your organic channels and your ads.

### Customer and creator UGC

Real customers tell their own story, with their permission. Where customers can't appear, vetted creators who match your audience present the service instead, briefed on what they can and can't say about it.

### Founder-led video

Founders and team members on camera, explaining the service and answering the questions customers ask most. In a founder-led business, the founder is often the person customers are deciding whether to trust.

### Short-form editing

Cuts, captions, on-screen text, sound and the opening hook, edited for Reels, TikTok and Shorts. AI helps us edit, caption and test faster, and a person reviews every edit before it goes out.

### Ad creative variations

One shoot becomes several ads. We cut each video into versions with different hooks, captions and calls to action, so paid social can test which one brings enquiries. We can also [run your UGC as Meta and TikTok ads](/services/digital-marketing/paid-social/).

## How a UGC project runs, step by step

A Linkclick Digital UGC project runs in seven steps, from a brief built around your goal to a report on which videos brought enquiries. You sign off scripts before filming and edits before anything is published, so if your industry has advertising rules, the checks are part of the plan rather than a last-minute hurdle.

1. **Brief.** We agree the goal (bookings, enquiry calls or sign-ups), the audience, the channels and how success is measured.
2. **Scripts and hooks.** We write each script with several opening hooks and check every claim against the advertising rules that apply to your business. A claims log records the evidence behind each claim.
3. **Casting.** We choose who appears and agree usage rights in writing.
4. **Filming.** Customers, creators and founders film to the approved script.
5. **Editing and variations.** We edit, caption and cut ad versions, and a person checks every cut.
6. **Sign-off.** You approve the edits. If your business has a compliance lead, they sign off before publication.
7. **Launch and learn.** Videos go to your channels and into paid tests. We report which hooks and creators brought enquiries, and the next batch builds on that.

## How much does UGC content cost?

UGC content costs more or less depending on four things: the usage rights you need, the number of hooks and edits per video, the creator's credentials and how fast you need it. Linkclick Digital doesn't publish fixed prices; we scope each project after a strategy call.

- **Usage rights.** Organic-only use costs less than paid-ad use; longer periods and more channels cost more.
- **Hooks and edits.** Each extra hook or ad version adds editing time, and gives your ads more to test.
- **Creator credentials.** A creator with a relevant professional qualification usually costs more than a general lifestyle creator.
- **Turnaround.** A rushed timeline narrows which creators are free and adds cost.

For UK creator rates and agency fees, see our guide to [what UGC costs in the UK in 2026](/insights/ugc-costs-uk/). To scope your project, [book a strategy call](/contact/) and choose a budget band on the form.

## Is UGC worth it?

UGC is worth it when each video answers a question that stops people booking, and when it is judged on enquiries rather than views. It is rarely worth it as a one-off video with no plan to test it. For founder-led businesses that sell on trust, showing the real people behind the service is part of the sale.

Spending on creator content is growing in the UK: advertiser investment in creator partnerships reached £966m in 2025 and is forecast to grow 26% to £1.217bn in 2026, according to [IAB UK figures reported by InPublishing](https://inpublishing.co.uk/articles/uk-creator-revenue-to-surpass-1bn-for-the-first-time-in-2026-26924). Video made up 59% of UK social media ad spend in 2025 ([IAB UK](https://www.iabuk.com/news-article/digital-adspend-2025-uks-digital-ad-market-reaches-ps405bn)).

Judge it on cost per enquiry or per booked call, against your current ads over the same period.

## Do AI UGC ads work?

AI UGC ads, which use AI-generated actors instead of real people, are cheap to make but carry a trust cost. In [YouGov's July 2026 survey of 2,012 UK adults](https://yougov.com/en-gb/articles/55180-half-of-britons-have-seen-ai-generated-actors-in-ads-and-they-dont-like-them), 78% said they would trust a brand less if it used AI-generated actors.

The [same YouGov survey](https://yougov.com/en-gb/articles/55180-half-of-britons-have-seen-ai-generated-actors-in-ads-and-they-dont-like-them) found that 69% had seen ads with AI-generated actors, and 76% of those who had seen them disliked them. It also found that 94% said companies should disclose when they use them.

Linkclick Digital's position is human-led and AI-assisted. Real people stay on camera, and we don't use AI actors to stand in for them. AI works behind the scenes on script variants, editing, captions and testing, and a person checks everything it touches. Where AI use shows on screen, we disclose it.

## UGC vs influencer marketing

UGC and influencer marketing both use creators, but they buy different things. With UGC, a brand pays for content and runs it on its own channels and in its own ads. With influencer marketing, a brand pays for a creator's audience, and the creator posts to their own followers. UGC is judged on the content; influencer posts on reach.

| Compared on | UGC | Influencer marketing |
|---|---|---|
| What you pay for | The videos and the right to use them | A post to the creator's followers |
| Where it appears | Your accounts and your paid ads | The creator's own account |
| How it's judged | Enquiries and cost per enquiry | Reach, engagement and referral traffic |

For most founder-led businesses, UGC is the better start: you control where the videos run, you can test them in ads, and results don't rest on one creator's audience. In the UK, when a creator posts paid brand content to their own followers, it must be clearly labelled as an ad, as the [ASA's influencer guide, written with the CMA](https://www.asa.org.uk/resource/influencers-guide.html), explains.

## UGC for businesses with advertising rules

UGC for businesses with advertising rules works when each script is built around what the rules allow, such as the person behind the service and the customer's own experience, rather than restricted claims. If your industry has advertising rules, Linkclick Digital checks every script, ad and edit against them before it goes live, and you keep final sign-off.

Some industries limit what an ad can claim, who can appear in it and what it must disclose. Linkclick Digital builds those checks into each project rather than leaving them to the end:

- **Claims.** Scripts only make claims you can back with evidence, and a claims log records the evidence behind each one.
- **People on camera.** Every customer, creator and founder is briefed on what they can and can't say about your product or service.
- **Disclosure.** Paid partnerships are labelled as ads, and where AI use shows on screen, we disclose it.
- **Sign-off.** You, or your compliance lead, approve scripts before filming and edits before anything is published.

Linkclick Digital is not a law firm. Where your rules call for a legal judgement, final approval stays with you and your own legal or compliance team.
