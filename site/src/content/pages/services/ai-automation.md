An AI automation agency designs, builds and runs the AI assistants and workflows that take repetitive work off a business, such as replying to enquiries, booking appointments and sending follow-ups. Linkclick Digital does this as a done-for-you service: it scopes the flows, writes the answers you approve, connects your tools, tests everything and keeps improving it after launch.

A done-for-you service closes that gap without turning your team into chatbot builders.

## Can AI book appointments?

Yes. An AI assistant connected to your calendar can answer an enquiry, ask the qualifying questions you choose, offer open slots, book the appointment and send confirmations and reminders. Linkclick Digital sets up booking flows so the assistant only offers the appointment types and times you allow, and passes anything unusual or sensitive to a person.

A typical booking conversation runs like this:

1. A customer messages your website chat, Instagram or WhatsApp, at any hour.
2. The assistant replies straight away, using only answers your team has approved.
3. It asks the qualifying questions you set, such as which service they need and where they are based.
4. It offers open slots from your calendar for the right appointment type.
5. It books the slot, sends a confirmation and adds the lead to your CRM.
6. It sends reminders before the appointment, and follows up with people who didn't book.

Where a question needs professional judgement, the assistant books a call or appointment with the right person on your team instead of answering it.

## AI receptionist and chat assistants for your website, Instagram and WhatsApp

An AI receptionist answers the questions your team answers every day, on the channels customers already use: a chat window or enquiry form on your website, Instagram direct messages and WhatsApp. Linkclick Digital builds one assistant with one set of approved answers across all three, so every customer gets the same information.

It handles the routine questions: what you offer, how the process works, opening hours, the area you cover, what to expect at a first appointment and whatever price guidance you choose to share. Everything else goes to your team.

### Website chat and enquiry forms

A website that converts still loses the booking if the reply takes a day. The assistant replies to chats and form submissions as they arrive, and works best on [a website and landing pages built to capture enquiries](/services/web-design/).

### Instagram direct messages

Enquiries from ads, stories and posts often arrive as direct messages. The assistant answers them, books where it can and passes the rest to your team. It pairs with [social media management with community replies](/services/digital-marketing/social-media-management/).

### WhatsApp

For businesses that already take enquiries on WhatsApp, the assistant answers, qualifies and books in the same thread through a WhatsApp Business account.

## Keeping a person in the loop

Keeping a person in the loop means the AI assistant handles routine questions and bookings, and hands everything else to your team with the conversation so far. Linkclick Digital writes the handover rules with you before launch: which topics the assistant must never answer, when it should offer a person, and how your team is alerted.

The rules Linkclick Digital builds into every assistant:

- **Approved answers only.** The assistant answers from content your team has signed off, and doesn't improvise on prices, services or products.
- **No professional advice.** Questions that need professional judgement, such as whether a service suits someone, go to a qualified person on your team.
- **Sensitive topics go straight to a person.** That covers complaints, anything urgent and anyone who seems vulnerable.
- **Open about being AI.** The assistant says it is an AI and offers a person whenever asked.
- **Handover where your team works.** The conversation and a short summary arrive in your existing inbox or team chat.
- **Regular review.** Answers are updated when your services, prices or rules change.

Linkclick Digital is not a law firm: your business keeps final sign-off on every answer the assistant can give.

## Data protection

AI assistants handle personal data, so data protection is designed in from the start. Linkclick Digital's approach is to collect only what a booking needs, tell people they are talking to an AI and how their data is used, limit who can see conversations, and keep data only as long as your business needs it. Your business sets these rules with us.

Before launch, your privacy notice is updated to explain the assistant, and every tool in the flow, from the AI model provider to the messaging platform, calendar and CRM, is listed with its data processing terms and where it stores data. Conversation records are kept for an agreed period, then deleted, and your data protection lead signs off the set-up.

Linkclick Digital builds with third-party AI tools of various kinds, chosen to fit each project and the systems you already use.

## How much do AI automation agencies charge?

AI automation agencies usually charge in two parts: a one-off build and ongoing running costs. What you pay depends on how many channels and flows the assistant covers, how many tools it connects to, how much approved content needs writing and reviewing, and how many conversations it handles each month. Linkclick Digital scopes each set-up instead of publishing fixed prices.

### What drives the build cost

- **Channels.** Website chat, enquiry forms, Instagram and WhatsApp each need set-up and testing.
- **Flows.** Replying, qualifying, booking, reminders and follow-ups are separate flows.
- **Connections.** Each calendar, CRM, inbox or team chat tool adds set-up work.
- **Approved content.** Answers are written, approved and, if your industry has rules on what you can say, reviewed by your compliance lead.

### What drives the running cost

- **Usage fees** from the AI model provider and some messaging platforms, which grow with conversation volume.
- **Monitoring and updates**, which can run within a monthly retainer.
