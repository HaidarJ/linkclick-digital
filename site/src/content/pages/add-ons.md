## Brand identity design

Brand identity design from Linkclick Digital covers the logo, typography, colour palette and guidelines that make a business look the same everywhere it appears. It helps most before a new website, a relaunch or a first round of ads, when every new asset would otherwise copy an identity you are about to change.

A brand refresh is worth doing when your current look no longer matches the business, when different suppliers have left you with several versions of the logo, or when you are about to spend on content and ads that will carry it for years. The guidelines cover how the identity works on screen, in video captions and on social posts, not just in print.

Brand identity pairs with [web design that turns visits into enquiries](/services/web-design/), so the new identity is built into the site from the start, and with [human-led UGC](/services/ugc-content/), so titles, captions and end cards match.

## Copywriting

Copywriting from Linkclick Digital means website, ad and email copy written for people first and for search second, in plain English. The add-on is for copy beyond what a core service needs to run, such as a full website rewrite, new service pages, campaign copy or a sequence of sales emails.

If your industry has advertising rules, copy has to meet them as well as read well. Every page, ad and email we write is checked against the rules that apply before it goes live. A claims log records the evidence behind each claim we write, and you, or your compliance lead, sign off before anything is published.

Copywriting pairs with [web design](/services/web-design/) for site copy, with [digital marketing](/services/digital-marketing/) for ad copy and pages written to rank, and with UGC for scripts and hooks.

## Email and SMS marketing

Email and SMS marketing from Linkclick Digital means automated welcome, nurture, reminder and rebooking flows for people who have asked to hear from you. It helps once enquiries are coming in: it keeps in touch with people who weren't ready to book and brings past customers back, without anyone on your team sending each message by hand.

The rules matter here. In the UK, the [ICO's guidance on electronic mail marketing](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guide-to-pecr/electronic-and-telephone-marketing/electronic-mail-marketing/) says you must not send marketing emails or texts to individuals without specific consent. A limited "soft opt-in" covers your own previous customers, but not new contacts, and it requires a clear chance to opt out in every message. The ICO notes this guidance is under review following the Data (Use and Access) Act. Wherever your customers are, we plan every flow around consent and opt-outs from the start.

Email and SMS pair with [AI automation that answers and books enquiries](/services/ai-automation/): the AI assistant replies and books, and the flows follow up with the people who didn't book yet.

## Analytics and reporting

Analytics and reporting from Linkclick Digital traces each enquiry and booking back to the channel, campaign or page that produced it, and shows the results in one dashboard. It helps when you run several channels and can't tell which one is paying for itself, or when your reports count clicks and likes but not customers.

Typical work includes conversion tracking on your website and forms, consistent campaign tagging, and a dashboard that joins ad, search, social and booking data. It can also separate visits that come from AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Perplexity. That traffic is still small but growing fast: AI platforms sent 0.32% of website traffic in 2026, up from 0.02% in 2024, across 101,574 sites ([SE Ranking](https://seranking.com/blog/ai-traffic-research-study/)).

Analytics and reporting pairs with [digital marketing run from one plan and one report](/services/digital-marketing/), and with AI automation, so bookings made by the assistant are counted against the source that brought them.

## AI team training

AI team training from Linkclick Digital is a set of practical workshops that get your team using AI tools safely in daily work, from drafting and research to content and customer replies. It helps when staff already use AI informally but nobody has agreed what is allowed, what must be checked and which data must never go in.

Most small firms in the UK already use AI: 54% of UK SMEs were actively adopting it in 2026, according to the [British Chambers of Commerce](https://www.staffingindustry.com/news/global-daily-news/ai-adoption-among-uk-smes-climbs-to-54-in-2026). Spending on skills is rising too: in [a Survation poll of 2,043 London business leaders for BusinessLDN](https://www.resultsense.com/news/2026-06-22-london-firms-ai-skills-gap/), a record 81% said they intend to increase training spend over the coming year.

Workshops are planned around your business and any rules your industry follows. If you handle sensitive client data, the workshop sets clear rules on what may go into AI tools and what AI must never write without a human check. AI team training pairs with [AI automation](/services/ai-automation/): once your team knows what AI can do safely, it is easier to decide what to automate.
