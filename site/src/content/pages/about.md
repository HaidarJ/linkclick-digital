## What is Linkclick Digital?

**Linkclick Digital** is a London-based digital agency that works with businesses worldwide in many industries, from healthcare to banking, turning attention into booked calls with UGC and creator content, digital marketing, web design and AI automation. Incorporated in September 2017, it works with founder-led businesses wherever they are based.

Clients work with one team, accountable for one plan, one report and the result, instead of four separate suppliers.

## How Linkclick Digital works

Linkclick Digital runs four services as one system, because most growth problems sit between the pieces. A strong video that sends people to a slow website wastes the ad spend, and a website that converts but takes a day to reply loses the booking. One team plans, makes and runs all four, so every view has somewhere to go.

Each service does one job in the sequence from first view to booked call:

1. **Earn trust.** [Human-led UGC and creator content](/services/ugc-content/) made with real customers, vetted creators and founders.
2. **Get seen.** [Digital marketing across paid social, paid search, SEO and social media](/services/digital-marketing/), measured on booked calls, not likes.
3. **Convert.** [Web design built to turn visits into enquiries](/services/web-design/), structured so Google and AI assistants understand it.
4. **Answer.** [AI automation that answers and books every enquiry](/services/ai-automation/), with a person in the loop for anything sensitive.

Most clients start with a strategy call, then a growth plan that audits content, ads, website, search visibility and enquiry handling, then a monthly retainer. Linkclick Digital builds compliance checks into how it plans, scripts and publishes: every script, ad and page is checked against the advertising rules that apply where it runs, such as the ASA and CAP rules in the UK, and against any rules for your industry, before anything goes live. Linkclick Digital is not a law firm, and clients keep final sign-off.

## Who Linkclick Digital works with

From its base in London, Linkclick Digital works with businesses worldwide, in many industries. Whatever you sell, the job is the same: earn trust, get seen, turn visits into enquiries and answer every enquiry fast. Each growth plan is shaped around your own buyers, the questions they ask and any advertising rules your industry has.

## How Linkclick Digital uses AI

Linkclick Digital uses AI to help draft, edit and test work faster, and people check and approve everything before it is published. AI never stands in for a real person: the customers, creators and founders in Linkclick Digital's videos are real people on camera, and AI works behind the scenes.

Where AI helps:

- script and hook variations for ads
- first drafts of copy
- video editing and captions
- testing which versions of an ad or page work best

What people do:

- check every fact, statistic and source
- check scripts and copy against the advertising rules that apply to them
- approve every script, page, ad and automated answer before it goes live
- send work to the client's own compliance lead for sign-off where their industry requires it

Paid partnerships and AI use are disclosed. The public expects it: 94% of UK adults want companies to disclose AI-generated actors in ads, according to a July 2026 YouGov survey ([YouGov](https://yougov.com/en-gb/articles/55180-half-of-britons-have-seen-ai-generated-actors-in-ads-and-they-dont-like-them)).

The same rules apply to this website. AI helped draft its copy, a person checked every fact and approved every page, and each statistic links to its source.
