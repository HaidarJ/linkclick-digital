## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Similar technologies, such as local storage and device fingerprinting, work in a comparable way, and this policy covers them too.

In the UK, cookies are covered by the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR). Under PECR, websites must tell you clearly which cookies they use and must ask for your consent before setting any that aren't strictly necessary. The ICO explains this in its [guide to cookies for the public](https://ico.org.uk/for-the-public/online/cookies/).

## How we ask for consent

When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept optional cookies. Choose **Accept all**, or **Manage cookie consent** to turn each category on or off, or to reject all optional cookies. Nothing optional is set until you choose, and no option is switched on for you.

Your choice is saved for six months, then we ask again. You can change it at any time from **Cookie settings** at the bottom of every page. If you turn a category off, we stop using it and delete the analytics cookies it set.

## Cookies on this website

| Cookie | Category | What it does | How long it lasts |
|---|---|---|---|
| `lc_consent` | Strictly necessary | Remembers your cookie choices, so the banner doesn't ask again on every page | 6 months |
| `_ga`, `_ga_<ID>` | Analytics (only if you accept) | Google Analytics: tells visits apart, so we can count visitors and see which pages they read | Up to 2 years |

Strictly necessary cookies don't need consent. Linkclick Digital uses Google Analytics: its script only loads, and its cookies are only set, after you accept analytics cookies.

Marketing cookies would measure whether our ads on platforms such as Google, Meta and TikTok lead to enquiries. None are in use at present. If we add any, we will list them here, and they will only be set if you accept marketing cookies.

The website's fonts are served from this website rather than a third-party font service, so no font provider receives details of your visit.

### Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, Inc. may log visitors' IP addresses for security. This is a server log, not a cookie. Our [privacy policy](/privacy/) explains how this data is handled.

## Services you choose to open

Some links on this website open other services, which have their own cookie policies. Those services set their own cookies, not this website.

- **WhatsApp links** open WhatsApp, run by Meta, when you choose to message us. WhatsApp's own cookie and privacy policies apply.

## Your browser settings

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Most browsers let you block all cookies, block only third-party cookies, or clear cookies when you close the browser. If you block the `lc_consent` cookie, the banner will ask for your choice again on each visit.

We may update this policy, for example if the website adds analytics. The latest version is always on this page. If you have a question about cookies or your data, read our [privacy policy](/privacy/) or get in touch through the [contact page](/contact/).
