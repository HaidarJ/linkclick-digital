## Who is responsible for your data

Linkclick Digital is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. That means Linkclick Digital decides how and why it is used. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Linkclick Digital.

- Legal entity: Linkclick Digital Limited, registered in England and Wales
- Company number: 10976392

To ask about this policy or your personal data, email [info@linkclickdigital.com](mailto:info@linkclickdigital.com).

## The personal data we collect

We collect the details you choose to give us, plus the technical data our website host records.

### When you send an enquiry

The enquiry form on the [contact page](/contact/) asks for:

- your name
- your work email address
- your phone number (optional)
- your business name
- your website
- your industry
- your budget band
- your message

### When we arrange a strategy call

"Book a strategy call" takes you to the enquiry form. After you send it, we contact you to arrange the call, and add it to Linkclick Digital's Google Calendar with your name, email address and the time agreed.

### When you message us on WhatsApp

WhatsApp links on this website open WhatsApp only when you choose to message us. If you do, we receive your phone number, your WhatsApp name and anything you send us.

### When you visit the website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, Inc. serves the website's files and may log visitors' IP addresses for security. The website sets one strictly necessary cookie to remember your cookie choices. Analytics and marketing cookies are only set if you accept them in the cookie banner, and you can change your choice at any time from "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page. Our [cookie policy](/cookies/) lists each cookie.

## How we use your data and our lawful bases

UK GDPR requires a lawful basis for each way we use personal data. We use yours for these purposes:

- **To reply to your enquiry and talk with you about working together.** Lawful basis: our legitimate interests in responding to business enquiries and, where you ask about our services, taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.
- **To arrange and hold your strategy call.** Lawful basis: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.
- **To reply to your WhatsApp messages.** Lawful basis: our legitimate interests in responding to people who contact us.
- **To keep the website secure.** Lawful basis: legitimate interests in protecting the website and its visitors from misuse.
- **To measure how the website is used, and whether our ads work, if you accept analytics or marketing cookies.** Lawful basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time from "Cookie settings". We use Google Analytics for this, and it only loads after you accept analytics cookies.
- **To meet our legal obligations.** Lawful basis: legal obligation, where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

Where we rely on legitimate interests, you have the right to object. See "Your rights" below.

## Who handles your data for us

Linkclick Digital uses these providers to run the website and handle enquiries.

### Google (Google Workspace)

The enquiry form sends your answers to a Google Apps Script web app run in Linkclick Digital's Google Workspace account. Each entry is stored in a Google Sheet and emailed to Linkclick Digital's Google Workspace inbox. Strategy calls we arrange are kept in Google Calendar, which is also part of Google Workspace.

### Google (Google Analytics), if you accept analytics cookies

If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics records how the website is used: the pages you visit, how you arrived, your device and browser, and your approximate location. We use this to see which pages help people. It doesn't load unless you accept, and you can turn it off at any time from "Cookie settings".

### GitHub (GitHub Pages)

GitHub, Inc. hosts and serves this website. It may log visitors' IP addresses to keep the service secure.

### Meta (WhatsApp), when you use it

If you choose to message us on WhatsApp, your messages are carried by WhatsApp, which is owned by Meta. WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy apply to how it handles your data.

We may also share personal data where the law requires it, for example in response to a lawful request from a regulator or the police.

## International transfers

Google, GitHub and Meta may store or process personal data outside the UK, including in the United States. When personal data leaves the UK, these providers rely on safeguards recognised under UK data protection law, such as the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, or standard contractual clauses with the UK International Data Transfer Addendum. You can ask us for more information about these safeguards.

## How long we keep your data

We keep personal data only for as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy.

We keep enquiries for 90 days and then delete them. This applies whether you contact us through the enquiry form or WhatsApp, and to the strategy calls we arrange with you.

Google Analytics keeps detailed analytics data, such as the visits linked to a device, for 14 months, then deletes it. Summary reports with no details about individual visitors can be kept for longer.

GitHub keeps its server logs under its own privacy statement.

## Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

- be told how your personal data is used, which is what this policy does
- get a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- have inaccurate data corrected
- have your data deleted, in some circumstances
- restrict how we use your data, in some circumstances
- object to our use of your data where we rely on legitimate interests
- receive your data in a portable format or have it sent to another organisation, in some circumstances
- withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent

To use any of these rights, email [info@linkclickdigital.com](mailto:info@linkclickdigital.com). You will not usually have to pay. We must reply within one month. For complex requests, or if you send several, we can extend this by up to two more months, and we will tell you if we need to.

## How to complain

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal data, please tell us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator: [make a complaint on the ICO website](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the website starts using a new service. The latest version is always on this page.
