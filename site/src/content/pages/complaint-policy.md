## Our commitment

Linkclick Digital is committed to excellent customer service and to good relationships with customers at every level of the business, from the top down. As a customer, you are entitled to make a complaint to us.

We have this policy so that every complaint is handled as efficiently and effectively as possible. It covers complaints made verbally and in writing. Our aim is to resolve your complaint as soon as possible.

## Our responsibilities

We will:

- provide an efficient, fair and structured way of handling complaints
- make our complaints process open to all customers, including customers with disabilities or other additional needs
- keep you informed of the progress of your complaint and the expected timeframe for resolving it
- review our complaints every quarter so we can improve our standard of customer service

## How we handle your complaint

When you make a complaint:

- we will acknowledge it by phone or in writing within 2 working days
- we will keep you informed of its progress, the actions we propose and the expected timeframe for resolving it
- we aim to resolve complaints promptly, and we will generally resolve a complaint within 30 calendar days
- complex complaints may take longer than 30 calendar days, and in these cases we will update you regularly on progress and the likely timeframe
- we will tell you the outcome of your complaint, and we will tell you in writing if you ask us to

## Step one: tell us about your complaint

If you have a complaint about any part of our service or your dealings with Linkclick Digital, please email [info@linkclickdigital.com](mailto:info@linkclickdigital.com). You can also reach us through the [contact page](/contact/) or the WhatsApp link on this website.

We aim to resolve most complaints during your first contact with us. When you discuss your complaint with us, we can help you clarify it and put it into words.

You can put your complaint in writing if you prefer. We will respond, and we will confirm any details in writing if you ask us to. You can also use any other contact method on this website, or ask us if there is another method you would like to use.

You can appoint an authorised representative or advocate to deal with us on your behalf. To do this, email [info@linkclickdigital.com](mailto:info@linkclickdigital.com) with their name and your confirmation that they can act for you.

## Step two: investigation and escalation

Our customer service management oversees all complaints. If your complaint is not resolved straight away, we may need to investigate it.

An investigation may take 15 working days or longer. If it takes longer, we will tell you the reason for the delay and the expected timeframe.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can ask our customer service management to escalate your complaint directly to senior management. We will then try to make a senior manager available to deal with your complaint as soon as possible, depending on availability.

## Step three: resolution

When your complaint is resolved, we will confirm this with you within 10 working days.

If you do not hear from us within that time, please email us again at [info@linkclickdigital.com](mailto:info@linkclickdigital.com), or contact us through the [contact page](/contact/) or on WhatsApp.

## Our working hours

We are open Monday to Friday, 10am to 5pm, and closed at weekends.
